## Lettre de motivation
Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Junior Business Data Analyst position in your Finance team in Lausanne. The role sits exactly where I like to work: between finance, product and commercial teams, turning data from several systems into decisions.

At Parfums Christian Dior, I designed and delivered, end to end, about fifteen Power BI dashboards built on some fifteen reference tables of up to one million rows, fed by SAP, SharePoint and Excel, with Google Cloud Platform. They are used by more than 50 key users, from operators to the Global Supply Chain Director, across three production sites, and save at least 1.5 hours on every new product launch. As a freelance data and finance consultant for Cogeco, I built the consolidated cash forecasting tool for a holding and its ten subsidiaries, and handled the whole client relationship, from proposal to delivery.

SQL is part of my toolkit: I optimised SQL queries during my internship at Asygn, and I recently built Trezo, a personal finance web app running on PostgreSQL with 19 SQL migrations, now used by five people. I am an engineer from Arts et Métiers and hold a Master of Entrepreneurship and Innovation (minor in Business Analytics) from UQ Business School. I am bilingual in French and English.

What draws me to PIX4D is the chance to support a product company's decisions with data, in a team where I can own analyses from start to finish and learn fast. I will be available from February 2027.

I would be glad to discuss how I could contribute to your Finance team.

Kind regards,
David Perkins
