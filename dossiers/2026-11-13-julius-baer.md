Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Junior Business Analyst – Banking Operations Transformation role in Zurich. A core banking migration succeeds as much through clean data and well-mapped processes as through the system itself, and that is the kind of work I have done so far.

Through the Arts et Métiers Junior Enterprise, I rebuilt the salary calculation for Courlancy Santé: 10,900 employees and a €34M payroll. At Parfums Christian Dior, I delivered end to end about fifteen Power BI dashboards on reference tables of up to one million rows, fed by SAP, SharePoint and Excel, with a Dev / Test / Prod cycle and access governance. I am comfortable with SQL, Excel and Power BI, and I like explaining things clearly: I have taught 40 private students and a business English course to 50 students, which helps when writing user guides and training materials.

Banking is one of my strongest interests, and I want to learn securities operations, trade settlement and corporate actions from the inside, alongside your subject matter experts. I am bilingual in English and French, and available from February 2027; I would be glad to discuss how this fits your timeline.

Kind regards,
David Perkins
