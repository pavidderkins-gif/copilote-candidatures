## Lettre de motivation
Dear Shotgun team,

I am applying for the Product Data Analyst position in the new Money squad. Reconciling PSP data with internal ledgers and designing data models for payments and payouts is a problem I have already tackled, on a smaller scale, in a product I built myself.

Trezo is a personal finance web app I designed and shipped on my own with Claude Code: TypeScript and PostgreSQL with 19 SQL migrations, bank synchronisation, automatic reconciliation between manual entries and bank transactions with discrepancy alerts, and categorisation learned from each user's history. I also wrote an MCP server so that an AI assistant can query the data, which is close to the context engineering work mentioned in the offer. At Parfums Christian Dior, I built on my own about fifteen Power BI dashboards on fifteen reference tables (up to one million rows) from SAP, GCP and SharePoint, with a Dev / Test / Prod cycle, used by more than 50 people.

I enjoy defining metrics, explaining them to non-data teams and making sure nobody makes a decision on unreliable numbers. I am an Arts et Métiers engineer, I hold a Master of Entrepreneurship and Innovation from UQ Business School, and I am fully bilingual in French and English.

I would love to help shape the Money squad from February 2027.

Kind regards,
David Perkins
