## Lettre de motivation
Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the VIE Global Battle Against Waste Program Coordinator position in Barcelona. The role combines what I enjoy most: working with manufacturing data, building tools people actually use, and helping teams across sites share what works.

At Parfums Christian Dior, I built on my own the Power BI tool that tracks the production of every new product across three sites (Chartres, Saint-Jean-de-Braye and Pudong). It runs on about fifteen reference tables fed by SAP, SharePoint and Excel, serves more than 50 users from operators to the Industrial Supply Chain Director, and saves at least 1.5 hours on each launch. To get there, I had to understand how each site worked, standardise the data and adapt the dashboards to very different users: the same challenges as redesigning the BAW self-assessment tool and deploying a best practices tracker across Danone sites.

I am also at ease with Excel, VBA and SharePoint, which I used to build a consolidated cash forecasting tool for a holding company and ten subsidiaries. I am an Arts et Métiers engineer, I hold a Master of Entrepreneurship and Innovation from UQ Business School, and I am fully bilingual in French and English.

I would be glad to contribute to the BAW community from February 2027.

Kind regards,
David Perkins
